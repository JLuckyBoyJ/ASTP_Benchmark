The `heuristics` function takes as input a directed distance matrix of an Asymmetric TSP instance, where distance_matrix[i][j] is the cost of travelling FROM city i TO city j and is generally NOT equal to distance_matrix[j][i]. It returns prior indicators of how promising it is to include each directed arc in a solution. The return is of the same shape as the input. An ant at city i picks its next city j with probability proportional to pheromone[i][j] ** alpha * heuristics[i][j] ** beta, so larger values make an arc more attractive. Values must be non-negative.
