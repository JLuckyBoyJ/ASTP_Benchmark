Task: Given the cost matrix of an Asymmetric Travelling Salesman Problem (ATSP) and the tour the local search is currently stuck in, design a strategy that updates the arc costs so the search escapes that local optimum, with the final goal of finding a closed tour of minimum total cost. The matrix is directed: travelling from city i to city j generally costs a different amount from travelling j to i, so d[i][j] != d[j][i] and any rule that treats the matrix as symmetric will penalise arcs the tour does not actually use. You should create a heuristic that updates the arc cost matrix.
