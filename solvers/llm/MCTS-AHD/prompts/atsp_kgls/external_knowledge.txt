- The seed rule is plain guided local search: badness = the arc's own cost. Anything that only rescales that vector changes nothing, because the search compares badness values against each other.
- Knowledge-guided local search scores an arc by combining several of its properties rather than by its length alone. Useful ingredients: how the arc compares with the cheapest arc leaving u and the cheapest arc entering v; the reverse cost distance_matrix[v][u]; how far u and v sit from the "centre" of the instance, which for a cost matrix can be approximated by the row and column means or by distance to the medoid city; and how much the arc's cost exceeds the median cost of the tour's arcs.
- The current tour and the penalty history are inputs for a reason. An arc that has already been penalised many times and keeps coming back may be genuinely necessary; an arc that has never been touched but sits between two expensive neighbourhoods may be the real problem.
- `iteration` allows the rule to change behaviour over time, for example by weighting structural knowledge early and raw cost later.
- The matrix is asymmetric: distance_matrix[u][v] and distance_matrix[v][u] are different arcs carrying different information.
